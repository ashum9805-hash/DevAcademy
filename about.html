DevAcademy started as a Grade 11 group project but evolved into a solo passion project that showcases the fundamentals of web development.

📖 The Story

What began as a group project assignment in Grade 11 became a personal mission. Due to circumstances that arose during the project, I took on the challenge of building DevAcademy entirely on my own — from concept to completion.

The platform was created to make web development accessible to beginners. It teaches the three core technologies of the web — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — in a structured, engaging way. Whether you're taking your first steps into coding or reviewing fundamentals, DevAcademy is here to guide you.
